  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="meta">Dual Time Watch Face (package <code>de.corepilots.dualtime</code>) · Last updated: 5 October 2026</p>

  <h2>Summary</h2>
  <p>Dual Time does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The watch face works entirely on your watch.</p>

  <h2>What the app does</h2>
  <p>Dual Time is a Wear OS watch face that shows the current time of your device’s time zone and the current time of a second city you choose, together with the difference between the two. It is built with the Watch Face Format and consists of resources only. It contains no executable code of its own.</p>

  <h2>Data we collect</h2>
  <p>None. The watch face has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party services. It has no internet access and does not communicate with any server.</p>

  <h2>Data processed on your device</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Time and time zone:</strong> the watch face reads the current time and your device’s time zone from Wear OS in order to display them. This data is used only to draw the screen and never leaves the watch.</li>
    <li><strong>Your settings:</strong> your choice of second city, accent color and automatic day/night mode is stored by Wear OS on your watch so the watch face can show it. We do not have access to these settings.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Permissions</h2>
  <p>The watch face requests no permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, health data, sensors, microphone, camera, storage or notifications.</p>

  <h2>Sharing and third parties</h2>
  <p>We do not share data with anyone, because we do not receive any. Wear OS and Google Play are provided by Google and are subject to Google’s own privacy policy. This includes installing the watch face and syncing watch face settings between your devices.</p>

  <h2>Children</h2>
  <p>The watch face does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.</p>

  <h2>Changes to this policy</h2>
  <p>If the watch face ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated before the change is released. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.</p>
